For each transaction, extract the relevant amount of USDC tokens involved in the operation from the 'input' field in hexadecimal format: for minting transactions, extract a 64-character substring starting from position 75; for burning transactions, extract a 64-character substring starting from position 11. Convert the extracted hexadecimal amount to a decimal number, divide by 1,000,000 to express it in millions, and assign a positive sign for minting transactions and a negative sign for burning transactions. Sum these amounts per day to obtain the daily net change in market value, format the amount in USD currency format, and order the results by date in descending order.
