-- Task: Generate a report that displays, for each customer and each month, the closing balance at the end of the month, the monthly change in their account, and the cumulative bank account balance up to that month. The closing balance is calculated as the cumulative sum of monthly changes. Monthly changes are determined by summing the transaction amounts from the "customer_transactions" table for each customer and month, where transaction amounts are positive for 'deposit' transactions, negative for 'withdrawal' and 'purchase' transactions, and zero for other types. Ensure that if a customer has no transactions in a given month, the monthly change is recorded as zero, and the closing balance for that month is still included in the output.
